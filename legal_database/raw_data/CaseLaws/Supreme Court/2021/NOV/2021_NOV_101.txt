                                                   VERSUS

     PRABHJEET SINGH CHANNA                                            Respondent



                                               O R D E R
I.A. No. 138381/2021 is filed in this Transfer Petition to the

     effect that the parties have already settled their dispute as per

     Settlement Agreement dated 12.02.2021 and therefore the petitioner

     did not seek to pursue the matter any further.
In the said   circumstances, the petitioner seeks permission

     for                 withdrawal of the Transfer Petition. I.A. No. 138381/2021 is

     allowed.
Accordingly, recording the factum of settlement of the dispute

     the Transfer Petition is dismissed as withdrawn.
