                                                     O R D E R
The judgment under review had considered all the circumstances on record

          and then found that the conviction and sentence ordered against accused Pradeep

          and accused Sandeep was justified but accused Krishna Devi and Ishwar were

          deserved benefit of doubt.
The grounds raised in the Review Petition do not make out any error apparent

          on record to justify interference. This Review Petition is, therefore, dismissed.
