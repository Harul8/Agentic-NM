                                                            JUDGMENT
VIKRAM NATH, J.
1.   In this case, orders were reserved on 18.01.2024,
                              leaving it open for the parties to move an appropriate
                              application within two weeks, in case any settlement
                              is arrived at. More than three months have passed;
                              however, no such application has been filed. We are
                              thus proceeding to decide the matter on its merits.
2.   The appellants are the owners of the property in
                              question. Under the leave and licence agreement, the
                              property in question was given to the respondent.
                              However, the appellants are alleged to have illegally,
