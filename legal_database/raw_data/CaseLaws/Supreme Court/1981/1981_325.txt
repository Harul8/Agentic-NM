JUDGMENT
 P.N. Bhagwati, J.
1. We are of the view that having regard to the circumstances in which the offence took place it is not possible to convict the appellant Jarnail Singh of the offence under Section 302 read with
Section 34
of the I.P.C. since there was no pre-concert between Sarwan Singh and the appellant nor a meeting of minds between them before the offence took place. We, therefore, set aside the conviction of the appellant under
Section 302
read with
Section 34
I.P.C, and since he merely gave a takwa blow on the ear and caused simple injuries, we alter his conviction to one under
Section 324
of the Indian Penal Code. We are told that the appellant has already been in jail for about 2 1/2 years and we, therefore, reduce the sentence imposed upon him to that already undergone by him and direct that he may be set a liberty forthwith.
