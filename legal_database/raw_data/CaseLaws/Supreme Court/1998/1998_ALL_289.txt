ORDER
1. We have considered the order made in Civil Appeal Nos. 1720-21 of 1990. The Judgment in
Bangalore Water Supply & Sewerage Board vs. A. Rajappa & Ors.
was delivered almost two decades ago and the law has since been amended pursuant to that judgment though the date of enforcement of the amendment has not been notified.
2. The judgment delivered by seven learned Judges of this Court in
Bangalore Water Supply
case does not, in our opinion, require any reconsideration on a reference being made by a two Judge Bench of this Court, which is bound by the judgment of the larger Bench.
3. The appeals, shall, therefore, be listed before the appropriate Bench for further proceedings.
