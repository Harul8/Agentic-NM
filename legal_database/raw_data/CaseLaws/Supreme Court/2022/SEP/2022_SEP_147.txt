                                                  O R D E R
This transfer petition has been filed by the petitioner-wife

     seeking transfer of the proceedings filed under
Section 9
of the

     Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 filed by the respondent-husband in the

     Court of Principal                     Judge, Family Court at Nashik Road, Nashik,

     Maharashtra to the Court of Principal Judge, Family Courts, South

     West District, Dwarka Court, Dwarka, New Delhi.
On 27.09.2021, notice was issued by this Court and an interim

     order was passed staying further proceedings pending before the

     Family Court, Nashik Road, Nashik, Maharashtra.
We have heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the

     record.
Considering the facts and circumstances on the record, we deem

     it appropriate to allow this transfer petition.
Consequently,   Civil   Petition    No.A-222/21,   titled    as   “Manoj

     Tukaram

     West District, Dwarka Court, Dwarka, New Delhi.

The   Family     Court    at   Dwarka    is   directed      to    explore    the
