                                          CIVIL ORIGINAL JURISDICTION

                Miscellaneous Application No. 221/2022 in T.P.(C) No. 2498/2019


     M/S. HIMALAYA DISTILLERIES LIMITED                                        Petitioner

                                                         VERSUS

     URMILA PRADHAN & ORS.                                                     Respondent(s)


                                                     O R D E R
On   1st   October,   2021,   the   present   Transfer   Petition      was

     disposed of by directing                       that the title suit subject matter          of

     this                 petition    shall    be   transferred   to   the   District   Court   at

     Siliguri, West Bengal.
There is a report submitted to this Court by the learned

     District Judge, Special Division-I, Sikkim stating that there is no

     District Judge at Siliguri and therefore, effect could not be given

     to the order of this Court dated 1st October, 2021.
The learned senior counsel appearing for the parties agree

     that there is a Court of Senior Civil Judge at Siliguri in West

     Bengal where the suit subject matter of this petition can be tried.
Accordingly, the order dated 1st October, 2021 is modified. It

     is                  directed that the suit shall be transferred to the Court of

     Senior Civil Judge at Siliguri, State of West Bengal.
With the above discussion, the misc. application is disposed

     of.
