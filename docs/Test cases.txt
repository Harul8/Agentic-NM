**1. Landlord Lockout vs Rent Default**
Initial:
`My landlord changed the lock while my belongings are still inside. I need urgent access back to the flat.`
Follow-up:
`I have the rent agreement, payment proof, WhatsApp messages, and photos of the lock. No court order or written notice was given.`
Last fact:
`There are no rent arrears at all.`

Watch for:
- should not drift into generic non-payment eviction
- final opinion should react to `no rent arrears`

**2. Real Rent Default Case**
Initial:
`My landlord is trying to evict me because I have not paid rent for four months.`
Follow-up:
`I received a written notice, and I admit the arrears, but I want time to pay.`
Last fact:
`I can pay the full arrears within a week.`

Watch for:
- here rent-default logic actually should activate
- should differ clearly from the lockout scenario

**3. Domestic Violence with Delayed Medical Record**
Initial:
`My husband assaulted me two days ago and I want legal help.`
Follow-up:
`I have bruises, photos, WhatsApp messages, and my sister saw me immediately after. I have not gone to the hospital yet.`
Last fact:
`I went for medical treatment today and now have a clinic record.`

Watch for:
- last fact should strengthen evidence posture
- final answer should materially change, not just repeat itself

**4. Cheque Bounce but Security Cheque Defense**
Initial:
`A cheque I gave has been presented and I received a legal notice.`
Follow-up:
`The cheque was given during a business deal, but I dispute the amount claimed.`
Last fact:
`It was only a security cheque and the underlying contract was never performed.`

Watch for:
- last fact should shift the legal framing
- should not stay generic “dishonoured cheque” only

**5. Property Sale Fraud**
Initial:
`Someone sold family property using forged papers and I want to stop further transactions.`
Follow-up:
`I have copies of the sale deed, older title records, and signatures that look different.`
Last fact:
`The mutation has not yet been completed.`

Watch for:
- last fact should affect urgency and relief posture
- retrieval should stay in property/fraud/civil-protection lane

**6. Job Termination with One Crucial Procedural Fact**
Initial:
`I was dismissed from service after a departmental inquiry.`
Follow-up:
`I received a charge sheet and replied, but the findings were against me.`
Last fact:
`The inquiry officer relied on witness statements that were never supplied to me.`

Watch for:
- final answer should become more procedural-fairness focused
- last fact should clearly matter

**7. Preventive Detention / Custody**
Initial:
`My brother has been detained and we are struggling to understand what can be done.`
Follow-up:
`The detention order was served, but the family did not receive all supporting papers immediately.`
Last fact:
`Some relied-on documents were given only after several days.`

Watch for:
- system should pick up procedural delay significance
- should not stay vague/general

**8. Motor Accident Claim with Liability Shift**
Initial:
`I was injured in a road accident and want compensation.`
Follow-up:
`I have medical records, FIR details, and repair bills.`
Last fact:
`The other driver did not have valid insurance on the date of the accident.`

Watch for:
- final answer should change meaningfully
- retrieval should remain accident/compensation focused

**9. Consumer Defect vs Warranty Expiry**
Initial:
`A machine I bought stopped working and the seller is refusing help.`
Follow-up:
`I have invoices, service chats, and a technician message saying the defect was likely present from the start.`
Last fact:
`The warranty expired one week before the breakdown.`

Watch for:
- answer should incorporate the timing nuance
- should not collapse into “warranty over, no claim”

**10. Police Inaction**
Initial:
`Police are refusing to register my complaint even though I have evidence.`
Follow-up:
`I have messages, photos, and witness details, and I visited the station twice.`
Last fact:
`I also sent a written complaint by email and speed post.`

Watch for:
- last fact should strengthen prior-actions posture
- final answer should respond to escalation-ready facts
